🎯 Quick Start Guide
1. Upload Plans — Drag a PDF onto the viewer or click Upload Plan
2. Set Scale — Click the Scale tool, click two known points on the plan, enter the distance
3. Measure — Use Linear for lengths (pipe, curb) or Area for surfaces (sod, concrete)
4. Count — Use the Count tool to click each tree, shrub, or fixture on the plan
5. Build Estimate — Measurements feed into line items with unit pricing
6. Generate Bid — Click Generate Bid to create a professional proposal
🤖 AI Features
AI Rename — Automatically reads title blocks and names all plan sheets
AI Catalog Search — Search for plants, materials, and products by description
Auto Takeoff — AI scans plan sheets and identifies items for measurement
Plant Counter — Vision AI counts and identifies plant symbols on planting plans
